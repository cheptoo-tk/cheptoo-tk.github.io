I'm Tony Cheptoo, a detection and response analyst based in Nairobi. I work day to day with Microsoft Sentinel, Defender and KQL, triaging and tuning detections across cloud environments.

This blog is where I build in public: detections for attacks on **identity** (Entra ID, OAuth, tokens) and **cloud data platforms** (Snowflake, Databricks, secret stores), each tested in my own lab, with the queries, the false positives and the limits written down.

Everything here uses lab or synthetic data. Nothing comes from client environments.
